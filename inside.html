🕊️ Someone I love is in jail or prison

When your partner, your child's parent or someone else you love is in jail or prison, or coming home: staying in touch, helping your kids, and help for you. Free, and no sign-up.

Where to start. Find out where your loved one is held and how that jail or prison handles visits, calls and money: each one sets its own rules. Read them before you pay or travel. Your state's prison system has its own search; for a county jail, call the county sheriff's office.

Nothing you type here is sent or saved.

Sesame Workshop

Made for ages up to about 6, in English and Spanish, with a workshop on taking care of yourself as the caregiver.

Next step: Start with "Coping With Incarceration" and the storybook "In My Family". Watch a video together before a first visit so your child knows what to expect.

Prison Fellowship (a different program from the Salvation Army's Angel Tree)

If your loved one is a veteran

VA has justice outreach specialists for veterans in jail and court, and re-entry specialists for veterans in prison.

Website

More about this: If your loved one is a veteran

Veteran Resource Guide (Veteran Home Guardians)

VA pay is cut during a long prison stay after a felony conviction, and a spouse, children or dependent parents can ask VA for part of the money it holds back. VA does not send it unless the family applies.

Next step: Read the guide's page on veterans in the justice system, then apply for the family's share on VA Form 21-0788.
