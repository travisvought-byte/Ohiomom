Privacy

What this site collects, what it does not, and the choices you have. Last updated November 23, 2026.

This page covers Yoga Mom Talk at www.yogamomtalk.com, including the OhioMom pages and the Ohio family guide. It is run by Travis and Deborah Vought.

Who hosts the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the internet address of each visitor for security purposes. The OhioMom pages also load a script from Wix, which runs their forms and may set a few cookies of its own. We do not control those. GitHub's and Wix's own privacy policies say what they collect.

Visit counting

We count visits with GoatCounter, a visit counter that does not use cookies and does not record who you are. For each page view it receives the address of the page (never a search or ZIP code you typed), the site that linked you here, your browser type and screen size, and a rough location (the country) worked out from your internet address. Your internet address itself is not stored. We use the totals to see which pages help people.

What stays on your device

  • The Yoga Mom Talk pages store nothing of their own on your device. Pages that show Google ads load Google's ad code, which can set its own cookies (see Advertising below). Nothing you type in a search box or ZIP code box is sent or saved.
  • The OhioMom pages remember the area you pick, and which quick polls you have answered, in this browser only. Wix may set a few cookies of its own on the OhioMom pages.
  • The Leave this site button takes you to a weather search. It cannot erase your browser history.

Sign-up and forms

  • There is no sign-up and no account.
  • The Yoga Mom Talk pages have no forms. The Report a problem page opens your own email app, and nothing is sent until you send it from there.
  • The OhioMom pages have a few short forms: Report a problem, the message box and quick polls on Talk to us, and a question at the bottom of some pages, "Did you find what you came for?" What you send goes to our account on Wix, and we read it there. With a poll answer, a message or that question we also receive the page you were on and, if you picked one, your Ohio area. We ask for an email address only if you want a reply. Sending one saves an anonymous visitor ID in your browser so Wix can deliver it.
  • Please leave out private details about your situation. These forms are not watched for emergencies.

Advertising

This site shows ads. Every ad is labeled "Advertisement" and kept apart from the resource listings. We use Google AdSense and ask Google to show only non-personalized ads, which are not chosen from your browsing on this or other sites. Google and its partners may still use cookies or similar technology to limit how often an ad is shown, to count ads and to stop fraud. Google's ad code never loads on the Find help, Christmas help or Worldwide pages, the OhioMom help pages, the family guide, or the About, Privacy, Terms and Report pages. You can turn off personalized ads at https://adssettings.google.com and learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads, or opt out of many networks at https://optout.aboutads.info.

An ad never changes what is listed or the order of the listings. How we take ads is on the About page.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is for parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Other sites

Listings and ads link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Questions

Write to travis@vethomeguard.org, or use Report a problem. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. The Terms of use cover using the site.
