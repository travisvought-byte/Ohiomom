Privacy

What this site collects, what it does not, and the choices you have. Last updated November 20, 2026.

This page covers Yoga Mom Talk at www.yogamomtalk.com, including the OhioMom pages and the Ohio family guide. It is run by Travis and Deborah Vought.

Who hosts the site

The site is hosted on Wix. Wix loads its own script on every page it serves and sets a few cookies of its own. We do not control those. Wix's own privacy policy says what Wix collects.

Visit counting

We count visits with GoatCounter, a visit counter that does not use cookies and does not record who you are. For each page view it receives the address of the page (never a search or ZIP code you typed), the site that linked you here, your browser type and screen size, and a rough location (the country) worked out from your internet address. Your internet address itself is not stored. We use the totals to see which pages help people. Wix may also keep its own statistics for the sites it hosts.

What stays on your device

  • Apart from Wix's own cookies, the Yoga Mom Talk pages store nothing on your device. Nothing you type in a search box or ZIP code box is sent or saved.
  • The OhioMom pages remember the area you pick, and which quick polls you have answered, in this browser only.
  • The Leave this site button takes you to a weather search. It cannot erase your browser history.

Sign-up and forms

  • There is no sign-up and no account.
  • The Yoga Mom Talk pages have no forms. The Report a problem page opens your own email app, and nothing is sent until you send it from there.
  • The OhioMom pages have a few short forms: Report a problem, the message box and quick polls on Talk to us, and a question at the bottom of some pages, "Did you find what you came for?" What you send goes to our account on Wix, and we read it there. With a poll answer, a message or that question we also receive the page you were on and, if you picked one, your Ohio area. We ask for an email address only if you want a reply. Sending one saves an anonymous visitor ID in your browser so Wix can deliver it.
  • Please leave out private details about your situation. These forms are not watched for emergencies.

Advertising

This site may show ads. Every ad is labeled "Advertisement" and kept apart from the resource listings. If we use an ad network such as Google AdSense, it and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at https://adssettings.google.com and learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads, or opt out of many networks at https://optout.aboutads.info.

An ad never changes what is listed or the order of the listings. How we take ads is on the About page.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is for parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Other sites

Listings and ads link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Questions

Write to travis@vethomeguard.org, or use Report a problem. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
