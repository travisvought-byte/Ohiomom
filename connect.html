MomCo (formerly MOPS): moms groups

MomCo

A nonprofit that gathers moms in small groups, in person and online. It began as Mothers of Preschoolers and has Christian roots, so ask a group how it runs before you go.

Use Find a Group to see meetups near you or online. Ask whether membership or a fee is needed.

MOMS Club: local chapters for at-home moms

International MOMS Club

A nonprofit with local chapters for at-home mothers. Chapters plan playgroups, outings, moms' nights out and service projects.

Open the chapter list and find the chapter for your area. Local chapters set their own dues, so ask before you join.

Feeding help and support lines from people who have been there.

La Leche League: breastfeeding support from other parents

La Leche League

Meetings and one-to-one help led by trained volunteer Leaders, who have breastfed their own babies. A map shows groups and Leaders near you and online.

Open the map, allow your location or search your area, and contact a Leader. Ask whether there is any cost.

Postpartum Support International

Postpartum Support International (PSI)

PSI has Support Coordinators in every US state who can talk with you about postpartum depression, anxiety and other struggles around pregnancy and birth, and point you to local help.

Call the HelpLine or open the PSI site and choose United States to find help in your area. This is not an emergency line. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

National Parent & Youth Helpline

Parents Anonymous

Free, confidential emotional support for parents and caregivers when parenting feels like too much. Trained specialists answer by call, text or live chat any hour.

Call, text or chat. You do not have to be in a crisis.
