Help for moms, anywhere in the world

Help for moms wherever you live. Pick your country for its own crisis lines, family payments and support, or use the help below, which works in any country. Free, private, no sign-up.

All countries

In danger right now?

Call your country's emergency number. Here are the numbers for the countries we cover so far.

  • Call 000Emergency in Australia: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 911Emergency in Canada: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in India: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Ireland: Police, fire and ambulance (999 works too). Free from any phone
  • Call 119Emergency in Jamaica: Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, dial 110
  • Call 999Emergency in Kenya: Police, fire and ambulance. 112 also works from a mobile
  • Call 911Emergency in Mexico: Police, fire and medical emergencies across the country
  • Call 111Emergency in New Zealand: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Nigeria: National emergency number, free from any network
  • Call 911Emergency in Philippines: Police, fire, medical and rescue
  • Call 10111Emergency in South Africa: Police. From a cellphone, 112. Ambulance, 10177
  • Call 999Emergency in United Kingdom: Police, fire and ambulance

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.
