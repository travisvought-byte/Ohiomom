Help for moms, anywhere in the world

Help for moms wherever you live. Pick your country for its own crisis lines, family payments and support, or use the help below, which works in any country. Free, private, no sign-up.

All countries

In danger right now?

Call your country's emergency number. Here are the numbers for the countries we cover so far.

  • Call 911Emergency in Argentina: Police or an emergency ambulance. In Buenos Aires city and some nearby areas, 107 also sends an ambulance
  • Call 000Emergency in Australia: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Austria: Single European emergency number. Also 133 for the police, 144 for an ambulance, 122 for the fire service
  • Call 919Emergency in The Bahamas: Police, fire and ambulance. 911 works too
  • Call 999Emergency in Bangladesh: Police, fire and ambulance. Free, any hour
  • Call 211Emergency in Barbados: Police. Ambulance, 511. Fire, 311
  • Call 112Emergency in Belgium: Urgent medical help or the fire service. For the police, call 101
  • Call 110Emergency in Bolivia: Police patrols (Radio Patrullas). For the fire service, call 119
  • Call 190Emergency in Brazil: Military Police, when someone is in danger right now. For an ambulance (SAMU), call 192
  • Call 911Emergency in Canada: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 133Emergency in Chile: Carabineros, the police. For an ambulance (SAMU), call 131
  • Call 123Emergency in Colombia: The national number for emergencies and safety
  • Call 911Emergency in Costa Rica: Any emergency. INAMU's free 9-1-1 line for women facing violence answers any hour, every day
  • Call 112Emergency in Croatia: Police, emergency medical help and fire brigade. Free, any hour; you can report in Croatian, English, German, Italian, Hungarian or Slovak
  • Call 112Emergency in Czech Republic: Single European emergency number. Also 155 for an ambulance, 158 for the police, 150 for the fire service. Free, any hour
  • Call 112Emergency in Denmark: Ambulance, fire and police. For non-urgent police matters, call 114
  • Call 911Emergency in Dominican Republic: Police, fire and ambulance through the national 9-1-1 system
  • Call 911Emergency in Ecuador: ECU 911: emergencies of every kind, including reports of violence
  • Call 122Emergency in Egypt: Police. For an ambulance, call 123; for the fire service, 180
  • Call 991Emergency in Ethiopia: Police, toll-free
  • Call 112Emergency in Finland: Police, ambulance, fire service and social services. Free to call
  • Call 112Emergency in France: Any emergency, free, any hour. Also 15 for medical help, 17 for police, 18 for fire
  • Call 112Emergency in Germany: Ambulance and fire. For the police, call 110
  • Call 112Emergency in Ghana: Police, fire and ambulance, on every mobile network
  • Call 112Emergency in Greece: The single European emergency number. Free, any hour
  • Call 110Emergency in Guatemala: Policía Nacional Civil (120 works too). Bomberos Voluntarios, 122. Bomberos Municipales, 123
  • Call 112Emergency in Hungary: The single European emergency number, for when you need the emergency services
  • Call 112Emergency in India: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Indonesia: The single emergency number for fires, accidents, health emergencies and threats to public safety. Free
  • Call 112Emergency in Ireland: Police, fire and ambulance (999 works too). Free from any phone
  • Call 100Emergency in Israel: Police. For an ambulance (Magen David Adom), call 101; for the fire department, 102
  • Call 112Emergency in Italy: The European emergency number, free from any phone
  • Call 119Emergency in Jamaica: Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, dial 110
  • Call 110Emergency in Japan: Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, call 119
  • Call 999Emergency in Kenya: Police, fire and ambulance. 112 also works from a mobile
  • Call 999Emergency in Malaysia: Police, ambulance, fire and civil defence. Free, even with no credit
  • Call 911Emergency in Mexico: Police, fire and medical emergencies across the country
  • Call 19Emergency in Morocco: Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, call 15
  • Call 100Emergency in Nepal: Police control
  • Call 112Emergency in Netherlands: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 111Emergency in New Zealand: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Nigeria: National emergency number, free from any network
  • Call 112Emergency in Norway: Police. For an ambulance or acute danger to life or health, call 113
  • Call 1122Emergency in Pakistan: Ambulance and rescue. Police, dial 15. Fire, dial 16
  • Call 911Emergency in Panama: The national emergency number, free from anywhere in the country, for when a life is in danger
  • Call 105Emergency in Peru: Police. For fire, call 116; for an ambulance (SAMU), call 106
  • Call 911Emergency in Philippines: Police, fire, medical and rescue
  • Call 112Emergency in Poland: Any emergency. For an ambulance you can also call 999
  • Call 112Emergency in Portugal: Any emergency, free, any hour, every day
  • Call 112Emergency in Romania: The European emergency number, free from a landline or mobile
  • Call 112Emergency in Rwanda: Emergency. For an ambulance, call 912
  • Call 911Emergency in Saudi Arabia: Ministry of Interior emergency and security reports. Police: 999; Saudi Red Crescent ambulance: 997; Civil Defense: 998
  • Call 999Emergency in Singapore: Police. For an ambulance or the fire service, dial 995
  • Call 10111Emergency in South Africa: Police. From a cellphone, 112. Ambulance, 10177
  • Call 112Emergency in South Korea: Police, with interpreters at set hours. For fire and rescue, call 119
  • Call 112Emergency in Spain: Any emergency, free from any phone, even with no credit or signal
  • Call 119Emergency in Sri Lanka: Police emergency, for anything that needs the police right away
  • Call 112Emergency in Sweden: Ambulance, police and rescue services
  • Call 112Emergency in Switzerland: Works with a foreign SIM too. Also 117 for the police, 144 for an ambulance, 118 for the fire service
  • Call 110Emergency in Taiwan: Police. For fire or emergency rescue, call 119
  • Call 112Emergency in Tanzania: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 191Emergency in Thailand: Emergency report. For medical emergencies (Narenthorn EMS Center), call 1669; for fire, 199
  • Call 999Emergency in Trinidad and Tobago: Police. Fire, 990. Ambulance, 811
  • Call 112Emergency in Turkey: Emergency Call Centre (Acil Çağrı Merkezi), when you are in immediate danger
  • Call 999Emergency in Uganda: National emergency number. 112 works too
  • Call 102Emergency in Ukraine: Police
  • Call 999Emergency in United Arab Emirates: Police. For an ambulance, call 998; for the fire department (Civil Defence), 997
  • Call 999Emergency in United Kingdom: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 911Emergency in Uruguay: Police. For a medical emergency, call 105
  • Call 113Emergency in Vietnam: Police. For a medical emergency, call 115; for fire and rescue, 114
  • Call 999Emergency in Zambia: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 112Emergency in Zimbabwe: Police, fire and ambulance from an Econet phone. On NetOne, dial 114
