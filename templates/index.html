Find help near you

Most help in Ohio is run by your county: benefits and child care through county Job and Family Services, disability services through the county board of developmental disabilities, and shelter through one county front door. County-by-county pages are being built. Until then, dial 211 and give your county, or use the county finders linked in the listings above.
