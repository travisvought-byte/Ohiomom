🎄 Christmas help for moms

Worried about Christmas for your kids this year? Here is where to ask for toys, a holiday meal and help with the heat bill. Free, and no sign-up here. Pick what you need, or search for your town.

Find your countyToys for Tots campaigns
Late in the season? Many Angel Tree sign-ups closed in the fall, and each Toys for Tots campaign closes on its own date, some at the end of November and some in December. Start with your area's Toys for Tots campaign today. If something has closed, dial 211 and ask: “What Christmas help is still open for my kids?” Never pay to sign up for free Christmas help.

Salvation Army Angel Tree and Christmas help

Many local Salvation Army offices give Christmas gifts, and some give a holiday food box, to children in families with low incomes.

Website

More about this: Salvation Army Angel Tree and Christmas help

The Salvation Army

Angel Tree usually serves children 12 and younger. Each office sets its own sign-up dates, and many close in the fall.

Next step: Enter your ZIP code at SAAngelTree.org. If your area is not on it, or sign-up has closed, call your nearest Salvation Army and ask whether any Christmas help, a waiting list or a food box is still open.

Find your local Salvation Army

USPS Operation Santa: a letter to Santa

Children and families write to Santa, and people across the country adopt the letters and send the gifts asked for.

Website

More about this: USPS Operation Santa: a letter to Santa

U.S. Postal Service

It is open to families in the continental U.S., Hawaii and Puerto Rico. Not every letter is adopted.

Next step: Include your first and last name, your full return address and the wishes in order. Mail it to Santa Claus, 123 Elf Road, North Pole, 88888 with a First-Class stamp, postmarked by December 5, 2026.

Checked November 20, 2026. Sources: 1 2

Angel Tree for children with a parent in prison

A local church delivers a Christmas gift to the child on behalf of their mom or dad in prison.

Website

More about this: Angel Tree for children with a parent in prison

Prison Fellowship (a different program from the Salvation Army's Angel Tree)

Next step: The parent in prison signs the children up on an application from the prison chaplain, sent out each summer. If you are raising the children, you can ask Prison Fellowship to mail an application to the parent. A church contacts you, usually October to December, if an application was received.

LIHEAP can help pay a share of your energy bills for households with low income.

Call 866-674-6327Website

More about this: Help with heating and cooling bills (LIHEAP)

Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

Rules and how to apply differ by state. Applying never costs a fee.

Next step: Call the National Energy Assistance Referral line, weekdays 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. Eastern, or visit EnergyHelp.us. Give your city, county and state.
