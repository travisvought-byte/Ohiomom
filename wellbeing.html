POEM: support for moms from moms

Mental Health America of Ohio

Support for mothers with depression or anxiety during pregnancy and after birth. You talk with another mom who has been through it, and peer support groups meet online and in person.

Next step: Call on a weekday during business hours and say you are looking for help for yourself or someone you love. Cincinnati: 513-202-4611. Dayton: 937-660-9220. This is not a crisis line.

Crisis lines and safety at home

Free and private, any hour. You do not have to give your name. In danger right now? Call 911.

If someone checks your phone or computer, use one they cannot reach, such as a library computer. (it does not erase your history).

Find your local domestic violence program

Ohio Domestic Violence Network (ODVN)

ODVN connects you to the domestic violence program that serves your county. Local programs offer shelter, safety planning, court advocates and support groups. Shelter addresses are kept private for safety.

Next step: Call the information and referral line, or use the "Get Help Now" list on the ODVN website to find your county program.

Protection orders: free form helper

Ohio Legal Help

A civil protection order is a court order telling an abuser to stay away. This free tool asks plain questions and fills in the Ohio court forms for you. Filing has risks, so talk with an advocate about a safety plan first.

Next step: Use the form helper on a safe device. You do not need a lawyer to file, but legal aid may represent you for free.
