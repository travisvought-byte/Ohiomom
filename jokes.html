Mom joke of the day

One mom joke a day, for the days that need one. Today's is on the home page. The ones that already ran are below, newest first.

  • December 16, 2026Every load of laundry goes in with twelve socks and comes out with eleven. I have stopped asking questions.
  • December 15, 2026A toddler’s “I do it myself” adds eleven minutes and a lot of pride.
  • December 14, 2026Yoga taught me to see the light in everyone. Motherhood taught me to see the light in the hallway that somebody left on.
  • December 13, 2026The floor is lava. The couch is a boat. I am, apparently, the snack bar.
  • December 12, 2026I don’t need an alarm clock. I have a small person who announces the sunrise.
  • December 11, 2026Mountain pose: stand tall and still. Also known as waiting for someone to find the other shoe.
  • December 10, 2026Mom math: to be somewhere by 9, start getting everyone ready at 7 and arrive at 9:20.
  • December 9, 2026My stretching routine: reaching under the car seat for a snack that fell in 2022.
  • December 8, 2026“Because I said so” is just mom for “trust the process.”
  • December 7, 2026“Happy baby” is a real yoga pose. Keeping a baby happy at 3 a.m. is a much more advanced practice.
  • December 6, 2026I can’t remember why I walked into this room, but I know every word of a cartoon theme song from three years ago.
  • December 5, 2026My yoga class said to bring a block. I brought forty. They were already in my bag, next to one tiny shoe and a banana.
  • December 4, 2026I finally found my inner peace. It was under the couch with the missing sock.
  • December 3, 2026Meditation is sitting quietly and noticing your thoughts. All of mine are, “Did I switch the laundry?”
  • December 2, 2026Nothing in the world is quieter than a toddler who has just found the markers.
  • December 1, 2026Deep breath in. Deep breath out. Repeat until the shoes are on.
  • November 30, 2026Tree pose is easy. Try it with a kid hanging off each branch.
  • November 29, 2026My toddler’s favorite food is whatever is on my plate.
  • November 28, 2026Savasana: lie still, eyes closed, do nothing. I have been practicing it at 6 a.m. for years. It’s called pretending to be asleep.
  • November 27, 2026Motherhood is finding a rock in the washer and knowing exactly whose pocket it came from.
  • November 26, 2026I asked for five minutes of peace. I got it, in installments.
  • November 25, 2026Yoga has warrior one, two and three. Moms have warrior four: “Who drew on the wall?”
  • November 24, 2026My kid can’t hear “shoes on” from two feet away, but can hear a snack wrapper from the next county.
  • November 23, 2026Laundry is the only workout where the weights multiply while you rest.
  • November 22, 2026Downward dog was going great until the actual dog joined in.
  • November 21, 2026Bedtime is at 8. Requests for water, one more story and the meaning of life begin at 8:01.
  • November 20, 2026Why are moms so good at balance poses? Years of carrying a kid, a bag and a conversation at the same time.
  • November 19, 2026Mom coffee comes in three temperatures: hot, cold, and found in the microwave at bedtime.
  • November 18, 2026My favorite yoga pose is child’s pose. My child’s favorite is climbing on my back while I do it.
  • November 17, 2026I headed out to yoga and said “namaste.” My toddler said, “No, mama stay.” She won.
