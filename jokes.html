Deborah’s mom jokes

For the days that need one. They take turns at the top of the home page, a new one every minute. Here they all are.

  1. I headed out to yoga and said “namaste.” My toddler said, “No, mama stay.” She won.
  2. My favorite yoga pose is child’s pose. My child’s favorite is climbing on my back while I do it.
  3. Mom coffee comes in three temperatures: hot, cold, and found in the microwave at bedtime.
  4. Why are moms so good at balance poses? Years of carrying a kid, a bag and a conversation at the same time.
  5. Bedtime is at 8. Requests for water, one more story and the meaning of life begin at 8:01.
  6. Downward dog was going great until the actual dog joined in.
  7. Laundry is the only workout where the weights multiply while you rest.
  8. My kid can’t hear “shoes on” from two feet away, but can hear a snack wrapper from the next county.
  9. Yoga has warrior one, two and three. Moms have warrior four: “Who drew on the wall?”
  10. I asked for five minutes of peace. I got it, in installments.
  11. Motherhood is finding a rock in the washer and knowing exactly whose pocket it came from.
  12. Savasana: lie still, eyes closed, do nothing. I have been practicing it at 6 a.m. for years. It’s called pretending to be asleep.
  13. My toddler’s favorite food is whatever is on my plate.
  14. Tree pose is easy. Try it with a kid hanging off each branch.
  15. Deep breath in. Deep breath out. Repeat until the shoes are on.
  16. Nothing in the world is quieter than a toddler who has just found the markers.
  17. Meditation is sitting quietly and noticing your thoughts. All of mine are, “Did I switch the laundry?”
  18. I finally found my inner peace. It was under the couch with the missing sock.
  19. My yoga class said to bring a block. I brought forty. They were already in my bag, next to one tiny shoe and a banana.
  20. I can’t remember why I walked into this room, but I know every word of a cartoon theme song from three years ago.
  21. “Happy baby” is a real yoga pose. Keeping a baby happy at 3 a.m. is a much more advanced practice.
  22. “Because I said so” is just mom for “trust the process.”
  23. My stretching routine: reaching under the car seat for a snack that fell in 2022.
  24. Mom math: to be somewhere by 9, start getting everyone ready at 7 and arrive at 9:20.
  25. Mountain pose: stand tall and still. Also known as waiting for someone to find the other shoe.
  26. I don’t need an alarm clock. I have a small person who announces the sunrise.
  27. The floor is lava. The couch is a boat. I am, apparently, the snack bar.
  28. Yoga taught me to see the light in everyone. Motherhood taught me to see the light in the hallway that somebody left on.
  29. A toddler’s “I do it myself” adds eleven minutes and a lot of pride.
  30. Every load of laundry goes in with twelve socks and comes out with eleven. I have stopped asking questions.
  31. My toddler saw me in tree pose and did the only sensible thing to do with a tree. She climbed it.
  32. I tried to tell a joke at dinner. I got as far as “So a mom walks into a…” before somebody needed ketchup.
  33. I said, “Five more minutes.” My oldest said, “Your average is twenty-two.” He had a chart.
  34. I own a sweater for every mood. Today’s mood is “this one was clean.”
  35. I counted to ten and went looking for my son. The plant was giggling.
  36. People ask how I stay so calm. I’m not calm. I’m in warrior two and I can’t feel my legs.
  37. I asked my daughter which shirt she wanted. She said, “The black one.” I held up nine.
  38. I told my kids I needed ten minutes for yoga. They heard, “Please bring me every question you have ever had.”
  39. Mountain pose: stand tall, be still, be the mountain. Nobody asks a mountain for a snack. I envy mountains.
  40. I asked my youngest to use her inside voice. She shouted, “I AM.”
  41. Yoga says to let go of what no longer serves you. I started with folding the fitted sheet.
  42. I asked my teenager what he wanted for dinner. He said, “Yes.”
  43. Triangle pose: one hand to the floor, one to the sky. I found a puzzle piece down there. It’s been missing since August.
  44. I moved the cookies to the top shelf. My son moved a chair.
  45. I do yoga for flexibility. So far the most flexible thing in this house is bedtime.
  46. I put on a pink sweater, pink leggings and pink socks. My daughter looked me over and said, “You missed a spot.”
  47. Easy pose is the one where you sit on the floor. It’s called easy because getting back up is the hard part.
  48. I rolled out my mat. Within a minute there were three kids and a stuffed rabbit on it. I was not one of them.
  49. I gave my toddler two choices for breakfast. She picked a third one, which I had not offered and do not have.
  50. Goddess pose makes me feel powerful. Then somebody hands me a cheese stick to open and I remember my real job.
  51. Breathe in for four. Hold for four. Out for four. Answer “What’s for dinner?” Repeat.
  52. I bought my son new shoes. By the time I found the receipt, they didn’t fit.
  53. Crescent moon is a side bend. I use it to look around the corner and see what just went quiet.
  54. I told my daughter her outfit was cute. She was crushed. She had been going for “haunting.”
  55. Someone asked how many sweaters I own. I said, “Enough.” That was not true. There is no such number.
  56. Star pose is arms wide, legs wide, taking up all the room you can. My youngest does it in the middle of my bed at 2 a.m.
  57. I started a gratitude journal. Day one: “Everyone is asleep.”
  58. My mat says “non-slip.” It has clearly never met a juice box.
  59. Big-toe pose is about reaching for something just out of your grasp. I trained for it with the TV remote.
  60. I wear pink so my kids can find me in a crowd. Mostly they use it to see me coming.
