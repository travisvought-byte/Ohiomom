Where these listings come from

  • Public housing and Section 8: HUD's list of public housing agencies, from its open data site. How vouchers work is on HUD's Housing Choice Voucher page. A county lists the agencies with their office there or named for it, then the nearest others in the state, then the state's own voucher program where it has one. Programs a service provider runs for one group of people are left out.
  • Housing counselors: HUD's list of approved housing counseling agencies, also on its housing counselor page and counselor search. Counselors are matched to a county by the ZIP code of their office, and nearby ones are shown by distance.
  • Free legal help: the Legal Services Corporation's list of the legal aid programs it funds and its map of the areas they serve, also searchable in its legal aid finder. What legal aid helps with is on its page on what legal aid does. A county is matched to the program whose service area holds it.
  • Health centers: the Health Resources and Services Administration's list of health center sites, also searchable in its Find a Health Center tool and data downloads. School, mobile-van and residential sites are left out.
  • School districts: the National Center for Education Statistics' list of public school districts, also searchable in its district search. What the row says about evaluations comes from the Center for Parent Information and Resources page on preschool special education and the federal rule on written notice. A district is listed in each county its boundary covers. Charter schools and high-school-only districts are left out.
  • Head Start: the Office of Head Start's list of open program locations, also searchable in its center locator and how to apply. Locations with no phone number in the list are left out.

Listings are copied from these federal lists by a program, not checked by phone, and the date of the copy is on each one. A Website button appears only when that website loaded in our automatic check. If something is wrong, tell us.

Nothing you type on this page is saved. A ZIP code is matched inside your browser, using a list your browser downloads for the first digit of the code.
