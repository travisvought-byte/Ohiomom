Help paying for child care

Ohio Department of Children and Youth

Publicly Funded Child Care helps working or in-school parents pay for licensed child care. Families generally qualify at or below 145% of the federal poverty level.

Next step: Apply through the same Ohio Benefits application used for food and Medicaid, online or by phone.

Child support: open or change a case

Ohio Office of Child Support

Your county Child Support Enforcement Agency can establish paternity, set up a support order, collect payments and review an order when circumstances change.

Next step: Start on the state child support website or call the customer service line to reach your county agency.
