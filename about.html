About Yoga Mom Talk

A place where moms can connect, with real help for the moms who need it.

Who runs it

We are Travis and Deborah Vought, and it is just the two of us. Deborah founded Yoga Mom Talk. It grows out of OhioMom, her Ohio hub for mothers, which is now the Ohio part of this site. The same approach now reaches every state. Travis looks after the website.

We have four kids. One has type 1 diabetes and two have autism, so ours is a neurodivergent family. We live in rural Ohio, and we are involved in community building.

It is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any podcast, show or organization named "Mom Talk".

How we choose listings

  • We list free or low-cost public programs, nonprofits and official locators. We do not list private businesses, and nobody pays to be listed.
  • Each listing links to the published sources it came from and shows the date it was checked.
  • Contacts come from official sources and are not confirmed by telephone. Phone numbers and websites change. If one is wrong, tell us.
  • Every link is checked automatically each month, and a person reviews any that fail.
  • Domestic violence shelter addresses are never listed. We point to the hotline or state coalition that places people safely.

What this site is not

It is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. It does not give legal, medical or financial advice, and it cannot sign you up for anything.

Advertise with us

Yoga Mom Talk and OhioMom may carry a few ads to help keep the site free. We welcome businesses and groups that serve families: family and parenting services, maternal and baby health, yoga and wellness, child care, and the like.

  • Every ad is labeled Advertisement and kept apart from the resource listings and the help lines.
  • An ad never changes what is listed, what a listing says or the order listings are in. Nobody can pay to be listed.
  • Ads are never placed inside a crisis or help-now section, and never on our pages for a place to stay tonight or for taking care of yourself.
  • We review every ad before it runs. We turn down ads that are misleading, and anything aimed at people in crisis.

To ask about advertising, email us at travis@vethomeguard.org. Tell us who you are and what you would like to show.

Contact us

Email travis@vethomeguard.org with a question, a correction, an idea for a listing or an advertising request. To fix one listing, Report a problem is quickest. We read every message, but this is not an emergency line.

Your privacy and safety

  • There is no sign-up, and the Yoga Mom Talk pages have no forms. The OhioMom pages have a few short forms for reports and questions; our Privacy page says what they send.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not record who you are. Nothing you type in a search or ZIP code box is sent.
  • The site may show ads. Each one is labeled Advertisement and kept apart from the listings. If we use Google AdSense, we ask it for non-personalized ads only, and its code stays off our help pages. Google may still set its own cookies; the Privacy page says more.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' internet addresses for security. The OhioMom pages also load a script from Wix, which runs their forms and may set a few cookies. We do not control those.
  • A Leave this site button is on every page. It takes you to a weather search and removes this page from your back button.
  • It cannot erase your browser history. If someone else may check your device, use a private or incognito window, or a device they cannot reach.
  • Sites we link to have their own privacy practices.
