Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

Servicio telefónico 0800 4141

A free, confidential national line, open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, for women over 18 living in Uruguay who face domestic violence.

Call 0800 4141Website

More about this: Servicio telefónico 0800 4141

Inmujeres, Ministerio de Desarrollo Social (MIDES)

It listens, advises and helps you find a way forward. Relatives and others who know about a case can call too.

Next step: Call 0800 4141 or *4141. Deaf women can make a video call on 092 626 928, Monday to Friday, 9am to 2pm. In danger right now, call 911.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Línea de Vida 0800 0767

A free suicide prevention line, open 24 hours across the country and answered by trained staff.

Call 0800 0767Website

More about this: Línea de Vida 0800 0767

Suicide prevention line, listed by the Ministerio de Salud Pública

It supports people in distress and people who want to help someone else.

Next step: Call 0800 0767 or *0767.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2

Línea Azul

INAU's free line to report that a child or teenager is being mistreated, abused or neglected.

Call 0800 5050Website

More about this: Línea Azul

Instituto del Niño y Adolescente del Uruguay (INAU)

Reports are anonymous.

Next step: Call 0800 5050 from any phone, or *5050 from a mobile: Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, weekends 9am to 7pm, working holidays 10am to 4pm. It is not an emergency service: in danger right now, call 911.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Asignación Familiar – Plan de Equidad

A payment for children and teenagers, pregnant women and disabled people in households in socioeconomic vulnerability.

Website

More about this: Asignación Familiar – Plan de Equidad

Banco de Previsión Social (BPS)

Children up to 5 need a health card, and from 5 a school certificate.

Next step: Apply in person at the BPS office in your department, with the ID cards of everyone in your household; if you are pregnant, bring your obstetric card. Questions: BPS on 0800 1997 (*1997 from a mobile), Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, or MIDES on 0800 7263, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1
