Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.5 options

National Anti-Violence and Sexual Harassment Helpline (NAVH)

A line open any hour to get help with, or report, family violence, abuse, neglect, sexual violence or sexual harassment.

Call 1800-777-0000Website

More about this: National Anti-Violence and Sexual Harassment Helpline (NAVH)

Ministry of Social and Family Development

Victims, family, neighbours and professionals can all call.

Next step: Call 1800-777-0000, or use the online report form. Airtime charges apply from a mobile.

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.

Call +1 202-501-4444Website

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.2 options

ComCare

Money help for families on a low income or in urgent need, from short-term help with living costs to help right away.

Call 1800-222-0000Website

More about this: ComCare

Ministry of Social and Family Development

Next step: Call the ComCare Hotline, open 7am to midnight every day, or visit a Social Service Office. For Singapore Citizens, and Permanent Residents living with a family member who is a citizen. Airtime charges apply from a mobile.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Baby Bonus

For each child who is a Singapore Citizen: a Cash Gift of $11,000 or more, paid in steps until age 6½, and a $5,000 grant into a Child Development Account, which the government also matches when you save.

Website

More about this: Baby Bonus

Ministry of Social and Family Development

Parents must be married for the Cash Gift.

Next step: Apply in the LifeSG app when you register the birth.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Legal help with civil cases, including divorce, for people with limited means and a reasonable case.

Website
