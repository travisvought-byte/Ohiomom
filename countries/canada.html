🇨🇦 Canada: help for moms

Raising children in Canada? If you or your child are in danger, call 911. Canada's own services come first, such as 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (crisis line), ShelterSafe: find a women's shelter (for urgent help) and Kids Help Phone (for urgent help). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Choose another country.

Nothing you type here is sent or saved.

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

Call or text 9-8-8 any hour, anywhere in Canada, if you are thinking about suicide or are worried about someone.

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.

Call +1 202-501-4444Website
