🇧🇪 Belgium: help for moms

Raising children in Belgium? If you or your child are in danger, call 112. Belgium's own services come first, such as 1712 (violence helpline), Écoute Violences Conjugales (partner violence line) and Zelfmoordlijn 1813 (suicide line (Dutch)). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Dutch, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Nothing you type here is sent or saved.

A free, anonymous line for questions about violence, answered by professional counsellors.

Call 1712Website

More about this: 1712

1712 helpline on violence

The call does not show on your phone bill.

Next step: Call 1712 on weekdays, 9am to 6pm. Chat Monday to Thursday, 1pm to 5pm and 6pm to 10pm, and Friday until 5pm, or email any time. French speakers in Brussels and Wallonia can also call Écoute Violences Conjugales on 0800 30 030.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Family allowances (allocations familiales, Groeipakket)

A monthly payment for each child.

Website

More about this: Family allowances (allocations familiales, Groeipakket)

Your region's family allowance fund

Where the child lives decides the amount and who pays: the Groeipakket in Flanders, a family allowance fund in Wallonia or Brussels, and the German-speaking Community in East Belgium.

Next step: In Wallonia, ask Famiwal for the birth allowance from the 24th week of pregnancy with a certificate from your doctor or midwife; it is paid at the earliest two months before the birth, and your family allowance file opens when you register the birth. Famiwal's free line is 0800 13 008.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2
