If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

Orange Support Centre

A free line for anyone facing abuse at home or sexual violence.

Call 0800 111 222

More about this: Orange Support Centre

Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection

You can talk it through, get legal help and help finding emergency shelter, and learn about your health and rights.

Next step: Call 0800 111 222. It is toll-free.

Free health insurance in pregnancy

Pregnant women can join the National Health Insurance Scheme without paying.

Website

More about this: Free health insurance in pregnancy

National Health Insurance Authority

Care in pregnancy under the scheme is free, including check-ups, lab tests, scans and emergency care.

Next step: Register in person at your district NHIS office. Bring your antenatal card or a note from your doctor or midwife.
