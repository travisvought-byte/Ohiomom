🇨🇱 Chile: help for moms

Raising children in Chile? If you or your child are in danger, call 133. Chile's own services come first, such as Fono 1455 (violence against women line), SAMU 131 (ambulance) and Línea *4141 (for urgent help). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Spanish, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

Fono 1455

A free national line for women who are facing, or have faced, gender-based violence, and for people who want to help them.

Call 1455

More about this: Fono 1455

Servicio Nacional de la Mujer y la Equidad de Género (SernamEG)

It offers guidance and psychological and legal support.

Next step: Call 1455, every day from 8am to midnight, holidays too, or send a WhatsApp to +56 9 9700 7000. In Spanish. For the police, call 133; Carabineros' family line is 149.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Subsidio Familiar (SUF)

$22,007 a month for each child under 18, for families in the most vulnerable 60% of the Registro Social de Hogares who have no social security.

Website

More about this: Subsidio Familiar (SUF)

Instituto de Previsión Social (IPS)

Pregnant women can apply for the maternal subsidy from the fifth month.

Next step: Apply at your municipality with your child's birth certificate, and their health check card (under 6) or school certificate (6 and over).
