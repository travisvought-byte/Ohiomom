🇨🇴 Colombia: help for moms

Raising children in Colombia? If you or your child are in danger, call 123. Colombia's own services come first, such as Línea 155 (violence against women line), Línea 106 (crisis line) and Línea 141 (child protection line). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Spanish, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Línea 141

A free national line, any hour, to protect children and teenagers: when a child is scared at home, being hurt, or going without food, school or health care.

Call 141Website

More about this: Línea 141

Instituto Colombiano de Bienestar Familiar (ICBF)
