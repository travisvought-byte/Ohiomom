Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Vaiko pinigai (child benefit)

A monthly payment for every child from birth to 18 (longer while still in general education), whatever the family's income: about €129.50 a month in 2026, with about €76.22 more for a family on a low income, with three or more children, or with a disabled child.

Website

More about this: Vaiko pinigai (child benefit)

Ministry of Social Security and Labour, through the municipalities

Next step: Apply once, online through the social support information system (SPIS) at spis.lt, or in writing at your municipality with your ID. Staff check the rest in public registers.

Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Lump-sum birth grant and pregnancy grant

A one-off grant of about €1,036 for each newborn.

Website

More about this: Lump-sum birth grant and pregnancy grant

Ministry of Social Security and Labour

A pregnant woman who is not working and cannot get maternity benefit can also get a one-off grant of about €476, paid 70 days before the due date.

Next step: The ministry's page lists both. Ask your municipality's social support department how to apply.
