🇵🇹 Portugal: help for moms

Raising children in Portugal? If you or your child are in danger, call 112. Portugal's own services come first, such as Serviço de Informação às Vítimas de Violência Doméstica (domestic violence line), SNS suicide prevention line 1411 (crisis line) and Linha de Apoio à Criança 116 111 (child helpline). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Portuguese, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Nothing you type here is sent or saved.

Serviço de Informação às Vítimas de Violência Doméstica

A free, anonymous and confidential information line for victims of domestic violence, open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Call 800 202 148Website

More about this: Serviço de Informação às Vítimas de Violência Doméstica

Comissão para a Cidadania e a Igualdade de Género (CIG)

Next step: Call 800 202 148, or send a free, confidential text message to 3060. In danger right now, call 112.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.2 options

Abono de família para crianças e jovens (child benefit)

A monthly payment to help with the cost of raising children, until they finish compulsory schooling.

Website

More about this: Abono de família para crianças e jovens (child benefit)

Segurança Social

The amount depends on household income, the child's age and how many children get it. Single-parent families can get more.

Next step: Apply online through Segurança Social Direta or in person at a Segurança Social office. Apply within 6 months of the birth and it is paid from the month after the baby was born.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2

Abono de família pré-natal (pregnancy allowance)

A monthly payment for pregnant women from the 13th week of pregnancy, for households under an income limit.

Website

More about this: Abono de família pré-natal (pregnancy allowance)

Segurança Social

Mothers who live alone or only with their children get 35% more.

Next step: Apply online through Segurança Social Direta, in person at a Segurança Social office or at a Loja do Cidadão. Online, you only need to allow your pregnancy certificate to be shared through SNS 24.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2
