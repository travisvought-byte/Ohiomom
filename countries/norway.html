Need help right now?

If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

  • Call 112Emergency in Norway: Police. For an ambulance or acute danger to life or health, call 113
  • Call 116 006Domestic violence line
  • Call 116 123Crisis line
  • Call 22 40 00 40Someone to talk to

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Barnetrygd (child benefit)

2,012 kroner a month for each child under 18, from February 2026.

Website

More about this: Barnetrygd (child benefit)

NAV

Families in Finnmark, Svalbard and some Nord-Troms municipalities get 512 kroner a month more.

Next step: For a child born in Norway it is usually paid to the mother automatically. You must apply in some cases, such as when the child was born abroad.

Helsestasjon (child health centre)

A free service with health checks, childhood vaccines, parenting advice and extra follow-up for children aged 0 to 5.

Website

More about this: Helsestasjon (child health centre)

Your municipality

Pregnant women get check-ups, and a midwife or health nurse visits new mothers and babies at home after the birth.

Next step: Contact the helsestasjon where you live. An interpreter is used when needed.
