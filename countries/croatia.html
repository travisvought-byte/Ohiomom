🇭🇷 Croatia: help for moms

Raising children in Croatia? If you or your child are in danger, call 112. Croatia's own services come first, such as SOS line for women and children (Ženska pomoć sada), 116 006 National call centre for victims of crime (victim support line) and Crisis and suicide prevention line (crisis line). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Croatian, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Need help right now?

If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

  • Call 112Emergency in Croatia: Police, emergency medical help and fire brigade. Free, any hour; you can report in Croatian, English, German, Italian, Hungarian or Slovak
  • Call 0800 655 222Domestic violence line
  • Call 116 006Victim support line
  • Call 01 2376 335Crisis line
  • Call 116 111Child helpline

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Doplatak za djecu (child allowance)

A monthly allowance for parents who support their children, if your household income per person stayed under the limit in the previous year (€618.02 a month, using the 2024 budget base).

Website

More about this: Doplatak za djecu (child allowance)

Croatian Pension Insurance Institute (HZMO)

You must have lived in Croatia for at least 3 years, with some exceptions for people under international protection.

Next step: Apply at your local HZMO office, online through HZMO's e-Usluge, or free at any Croatian post office. The amount depends on your income; HZMO's website has the current tables.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Novčana potpora za novorođeno dijete (newborn grant)

A one-off payment of €618.02 from the state budget for each newborn.

Website

More about this: Novčana potpora za novorođeno dijete (newborn grant)

Croatian Health Insurance Fund (HZZO)

You need health insurance and must have lived in Croatia for at least 12 months (5 years if you are outside the employment system); the baby must be registered and insured at your address.

Next step: Apply within six months of the birth, when you register the baby at the registry office (matični ured) or at your HZZO regional office. Your city or municipality may pay its own newborn grant too: ask them.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1
