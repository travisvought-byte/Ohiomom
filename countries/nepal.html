Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Safe Motherhood Programme (Aama)

Giving birth at a public health facility is free: user fees for all delivery care were removed.

Website

More about this: Safe Motherhood Programme (Aama)

Ministry of Health and Population

You get cash for transport right after the birth (NPR 1,500 in mountain districts, NPR 1,000 in hill districts, NPR 500 in the Terai), and NPR 400 for completing four antenatal check-ups, at 4, 6, 8 and 9 months.

Next step: Give birth at a public health facility with a birthing centre and bring a copy of your Nepali citizenship. Ask your nearest health post, primary health care centre, district hospital or district health office.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1
