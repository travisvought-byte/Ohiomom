Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Barnabætur (child benefits)

A payment for parents of children under 18, worked out from the income on your tax return, with a little more for children under 7.

Website

More about this: Barnabætur (child benefits)

Iceland Revenue and Customs (Skatturinn)

It is paid four times a year.

Next step: You do not need to apply: it is worked out from your tax return. For the year your baby is born, it is paid by itself.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Prenatal care at your health centre

Pregnancy check-ups with midwives and doctors at your local health centre, and a postnatal check 6 to 10 weeks after the birth.

Website

More about this: Prenatal care at your health centre

Icelandic health centres (heilsugæsla)

Prenatal care is free once your legal home has been in Iceland for six months, and so is giving birth under national health insurance.

Next step: Book your first check between weeks 8 and 12 of the pregnancy at your local health centre.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1
