Need help right now?

If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

  • Call 112Emergency in Spain: Any emergency, free from any phone, even with no credit or signal
  • Call 016Violence against women line
  • Call 024Crisis line
  • Call 900 20 20 10Child helpline

Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

016

Free, confidential help any hour for women facing any form of violence, and for the people around them.

Call 016Website

More about this: 016

Ministry of Equality

Next step: Call 016, or send a WhatsApp message (no calls) to 600 000 016. The phone line works in 53 languages. You can also email 016-online@igualdad.gob.es.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

024 suicide prevention line

Free, confidential and open every hour of the year, for anyone with thoughts of suicide and for their family and friends.

Call 024Website

More about this: 024 suicide prevention line

Ministry of Health

Next step: Call 024 from anywhere in Spain. There is also a chat run by the Red Cross and a sign language video service. If a life is in danger right now, call 112.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.

Call +1 202-501-4444Website

More about this: U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

U.S. Department of State

Next step: Contact the nearest U.S. embassy, or call any hour. From the U.S. and Canada, call 1-888-407-4747.

Phone: From outside the U.S. and Canada, any hour

Social Security (INSS)

Families on it with children also get a child supplement.
