Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.5 options

SOS line 15900

A free line for women facing violence, open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year: counselling and psychological support, basic legal information, and referrals to other services.

Call 15900Website

More about this: SOS line 15900

Ministry of Social Cohesion and Family

The line can call you back if you ask.

Next step: Call 15900, or email sos15900@isotita.gr. The line serves women; for other cases of violence, EKKA's 24-hour social support line is 197. In danger right now, call 112.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

SOS 1056

The national SOS line for children, open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, answered by social workers and psychologists.

Call 1056Website

More about this: SOS 1056

The Smile of the Child (Το Χαμόγελο του Παιδιού)

Any child or adult can call, including to report a child at risk, with or without giving a name.

Next step: Call 1056, free from a landline or mobile, even with no credit. Arabic interpretation is available. Children can also use Chat 1056, any hour.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Επίδομα παιδιού (child benefit)

A monthly payment for families with children, set by family income.

Website

More about this: Επίδομα παιδιού (child benefit)

OPEKA

At the lowest income level it is €70 a month for each of the first two children and €140 for each child from the third. You must have lived in Greece for the past five years, or the past 12 years if you are not a Greek citizen.

Next step: Apply online once a year on gov.gr with your TAXISnet login. Applications open in early March and close on 15 January of the next year. It is paid every two months.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Επίδομα γέννησης (birth allowance)

€2,000 for each child born alive in Greece, paid in two instalments of €1,000, for families with income up to €40,000 a year.

Website

More about this: Επίδομα γέννησης (birth allowance)

OPEKA

The mother must live permanently and legally in Greece; mothers from outside the EU must have lived there legally for the past 12 years.

Next step: Apply within three months of the birth: at the maternity hospital together with the birth declaration, on epidomagennisis.gr, or at a municipal Community Centre (Κέντρο Κοινότητας). You need a mobile number, an email address and a bank account (IBAN) in your name.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2
