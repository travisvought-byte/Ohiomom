Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

OKIT (National Crisis Management and Information Telephone Service)

A free line, open every day of the year, any hour, for people facing violence in a relationship or the family, and for victims of human trafficking.

Call 06 80 20 55 20Website

More about this: OKIT (National Crisis Management and Information Telephone Service)

Országos Kríziskezelő és Információs Telefonszolgálat

It gives advice and can refer you to crisis care and a safe place to stay.

Next step: Call 06 80 20 55 20 from any phone in Hungary, or email okit@segelyszervezet.hu. In a life-threatening situation, call 112.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Áldozatsegítő Vonal (Victim Support Line)

A free line for victims of crime.

Call 06 80 225 225Website

More about this: Áldozatsegítő Vonal (Victim Support Line)

Government offices' victim support services (kormányhivatalok)

Every county government office also runs a victim support service you can visit, and the Ministry of Justice runs victim support centres you can visit wherever you live.

Next step: Call 06 80 225 225 free, or visit the victim support service at your county government office.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Kék Vonal 116 111

A free, anonymous line, open any hour, for children and young people under 24 who want to talk.

Call 116 111Website

More about this: Kék Vonal 116 111

Kék Vonal Gyermekkrízis Alapítvány (Kék Vonal Child Crisis Foundation)

Adults who are worried about a child have their own free, anonymous line, open any hour.

Next step: Children and young people call 116 111, or chat from 1pm to 9pm on kek-vonal.hu. Parents and professionals worried about a child call 116 000.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

Családi pótlék (family allowance)

A monthly payment for parents and guardians raising a child at home, and for young people still in school.

Website

More about this: Családi pótlék (family allowance)

Government offices (kormányhivatalok) and the Hungarian State Treasury

The government office leaflet gives 12,200 Ft a month for one child, or 13,700 Ft for a single parent, with more for each child in larger families and for a child with a long-term illness or severe disability.

Next step: Apply with the form 'Kérelem családi pótlék megállapítására' and a copy of your ID, online through the State Treasury's family benefits portal or at your government office. It is paid from the date you apply, and can go back to the first day of the second month before.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1

Pregnancy and new baby

Breastfeeding help, maternity benefits and support with a new baby.3 options

Anyasági támogatás (maternity support)

A one-off payment after the birth for a mother who went to at least four prenatal care visits (one is enough if the baby came early).

Website

More about this: Anyasági támogatás (maternity support)

Government offices (kormányhivatalok) and the Hungarian State Treasury

The government office leaflet gives 64,125 Ft for one child and 85,500 Ft for each twin.

Next step: Apply within six months of the birth with the form 'Kérelem anyasági támogatás megállapítására', a copy of your ID and a note from your obstetrician, midwife or health visitor (védőnő) confirming your prenatal visits. Hand it to your employer's family benefits office if it has one, or to your county or Budapest government office.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1
