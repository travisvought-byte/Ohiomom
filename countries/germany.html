Pick what you need, or search every listing. Each choice shows Germany's own services first, then help that works in any country. Choose another country.

Need help right now?

If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

  • Call 112Emergency in Germany: Ambulance and fire. For the police, call 110
  • Call 116 016Violence against women line
  • Call 0800 111 0 111Crisis line
  • Call 0800 40 40 020Pregnancy help line

Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

Hilfetelefon Gewalt gegen Frauen

A free, anonymous national line for women facing any kind of violence, open around the clock, every day of the year.

Call 116 016Website

More about this: Hilfetelefon Gewalt gegen Frauen

Federal Office for Family and Civil Society Tasks (BAFzA)

Family, friends and professionals can call too.

Next step: Call 116 016. Help is in German and 18 other languages with interpreters, in German Sign Language and in easy German. You can also get help online on the website.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2

TelefonSeelsorge

Someone to talk to, free and anonymous, day and night, whatever is weighing on you.

Call 0800 111 0 111Website

More about this: TelefonSeelsorge

TelefonSeelsorge (run by the churches)

You can also write by chat or email, or talk face to face at a local centre.

Next step: Call 0800 111 0 111, 0800 111 0 222 or 116 123. The line is in German.

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.

Call +1 202-501-4444Website

More about this: U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

U.S. Department of State

Next step: Contact the nearest U.S. embassy, or call any hour. From the U.S. and Canada, call 1-888-407-4747.

Phone: From outside the U.S. and Canada, any hour

Call 0800 40 40 020Website

Federal Ministry for Family Affairs

It can also help you find a pregnancy counselling centre near you.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.2 options

Kindergeld (child benefit)

€259 a month for each child from January 2026, whatever your income.

Website

More about this: Kindergeld (child benefit)

Familienkasse, Federal Employment Agency

It goes to the parent the child lives with.

Next step: It is paid by the Familienkasse at the Agentur für Arbeit. Families on a low income can also get Kinderzuschlag, up to €297 a month per child on top, and can apply online with a BundID account.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2

Bundesstiftung Mutter und Kind

Money for pregnant women in financial hardship, for things like maternity clothes, the baby's first things, the home and childcare.

Website

More about this: Bundesstiftung Mutter und Kind

Federal Foundation Mother and Child

It is not taken off Bürgergeld or other benefits.

Next step: Apply at a pregnancy counselling centre (Schwangerschaftsberatungsstelle) near you, before the baby is born. Counselling there is free and can be anonymous.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2
