🇳🇱 Netherlands: help for moms

Raising children in the Netherlands? If you or your child are in danger, call 112. The Netherlands' own services come first, such as Veilig Thuis (family violence line), 113 Zelfmoordpreventie (crisis line) and De Kindertelefoon (child helpline). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Today's kind word, near the end of the page, is in Dutch, with the English under it. Choose another country.

Need help right now?

If you or your child are in danger, call 112.

  • Call 112Emergency in the Netherlands: Police, fire and ambulance
  • Call 0800-2000Family violence line
  • Call 113Crisis line
  • Call 0800-0432Child helpline

Sociale Verzekeringsbank (SVB)

Next step: Register your baby's birth with your municipality. The SVB then sends you a letter within a few weeks, and you apply online in My SVB with DigiD or ask for a paper form. Register your baby with your health insurer within 4 months too.
