Get help now

Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.7 options

DV相談＋ (DV Hotline Plus)

Help with domestic violence by phone, any hour of the day, and by chat from noon to 10pm.

Call 0120-279-889Website

More about this: DV相談＋ (DV Hotline Plus)

Gender Equality Bureau, Cabinet Office

Next step: Call 0120-279-889. Phone help is in Japanese only. Chat is in 10 other languages: English, Tagalog, Thai, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese, Nepali, Vietnamese and Indonesian. You can also dial #8008 to reach your prefecture's spousal violence support centre in its opening hours (normal call charges).

Child abuse line 189

If you think a child may be being abused, call 189.

Call 189Website

More about this: Child abuse line 189

Children and Families Agency

It puts you through quickly, 24 hours a day from anywhere in Japan, to the local child guidance centre.

Next step: Call 189 (some IP phones cannot connect). For worries about pregnancy, birth or raising your child, the child guidance centres have a toll-free line: 0120-189-783.

Food and bills

Family payments, child benefits and one number that finds local help.1 option

児童手当 (child allowance)

Paid for every child up to high-school age, whatever your income: ¥15,000 a month under age 3 and ¥10,000 a month from age 3, or ¥30,000 a month for a third child or later.

Website

More about this: 児童手当 (child allowance)

Your city, ward or town office

It is paid six times a year.

Next step: Hand in a claim form (児童手当認定請求書) at your municipal office soon after the birth, since payment starts the month after you apply. Public employees apply at their workplace.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 1 2
