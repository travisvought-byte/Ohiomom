Safety, crisis support and urgent needs. Free, and most lines are open any hour.6 options

1938 Women Help Line

A toll-free helpline for women, open 24 hours every day in all three of the country's languages.

Call 1938Website

More about this: 1938 Women Help Line

Ministry of Women and Child Affairs

It takes complaints of violence, gives counselling and legal advice, follows up, and refers you to other services.

Next step: Call 1938. In danger right now, call the police on 119.

1929 ChildLine

A free, confidential line, open any hour, every day, for any child or adult to report child abuse or ask about a child.

Call 1929Website

More about this: 1929 ChildLine

National Child Protection Authority (NCPA)

It also connects children with longer-term care. In Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Next step: Call 1929 free from any network.
