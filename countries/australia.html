🇦🇺 Australia: help for moms

Raising children in Australia? If you or your child are in danger, call 000. Australia's own services come first, such as 1800RESPECT (family violence line), Lifeline crisis support (crisis line) and PANDA perinatal mental health helpline (when you need someone to talk to). Then come lines that work in any country. Pick what you need, or search. Choose another country.

Australia's national counselling and support line for domestic, family and sexual violence.

U.S. citizens abroad: emergency help

For American moms living or traveling outside the U.S. The State Department helps U.S. citizens abroad with emergencies, including crime, arrest, lost passports and a parent taking a child across a border.
