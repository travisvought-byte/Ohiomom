Get help now

Domestic violence, crisis support and protection orders.

In danger right now? Call 911.

If someone checks your phone or computer, use a device they cannot reach, such as one at a library or a friend's home. The "Leave this site" button at the top of every page takes you to a weather search right away. It does not erase your browser history.

National Domestic Violence Hotline

National Domestic Violence Hotline · National

Free, private support any hour of the day. An advocate can help you think through safety, shelter and next steps, whether or not you are ready to leave.

Next step: Call, text or chat. You do not have to give your name.

Call 800-799-7233Text START to 88788Website

Find your local domestic violence program

Ohio Domestic Violence Network (ODVN)

ODVN connects you to the domestic violence program that serves your county. Local programs offer shelter, safety planning, court advocates and support groups. Shelter addresses are kept private for safety.

Next step: Call the information and referral line, or use the "Get Help Now" list on the ODVN website to find your county program.

Protection orders: free form helper

Ohio Legal Help

A civil protection order is a court order telling an abuser to stay away. This free tool asks plain questions and fills in the Ohio court forms for you. Filing has risks, so talk with an advocate about a safety plan first.

Next step: Use the form helper on a safe device. You do not need a lawyer to file, but legal aid may represent you for free.

Choices besides guardianship at 18

National Resource Center for Supported Decision-Making · National

At 18, your child legally makes their own decisions. Guardianship is not the only option. Supported decision-making lets your child choose trusted people to help with decisions while keeping their rights.

Next step: Read about the options before you go to court. Ask your parent center or disability rights agency for help.

Children's hospital evaluations for autism and development

Ohio children's hospitals

Ohio's large children's hospitals have teams that check children for autism, developmental delay and complex ADHD. These visits often have a wait of several months, so it helps to get on the list early.

Next step: Ask your child's doctor for a referral. Most hospitals then call you to set the visit. Keep using Help Me Grow or the school while you wait. Your child does not need a medical diagnosis to get those services.

Youth mobile crisis team (MRSS)

Ohio Department of Behavioral Health and Ohio Medicaid

Free crisis help for anyone 20 or younger in any Ohio county. On weekdays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., a trained team usually comes within an hour to your home, school or another safe place. At other times, a counselor helps by phone or video and a team visits the next day. You decide if it's a crisis.

Next step: Call or text 988 and ask for MRSS. If anyone is in physical danger, call 911.

Find help near you

Most help in Ohio is run by your county: benefits and child care through county Job and Family Services, disability services through the county board of developmental disabilities, and shelter through one county front door.
