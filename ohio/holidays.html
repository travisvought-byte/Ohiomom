Holidays in Ohio

Help with gifts, holiday meals and the heat bill, and lights, Santa and parades close to home.

Help this holiday season

Sign-ups for Christmas help close early, often in November. If one has closed, dial 211 and ask what is still open in your county.

Toys for Tots: ask for toys for your kids

Marine Toys for Tots gives new toys to children whose families cannot afford Christmas gifts.

Website

More about this: Toys for Tots: ask for toys for your kids

Marine Toys for Tots

Each county or area runs its own campaign on its own dates. Some Ohio campaigns stopped taking requests in early November (Cincinnati's closed November 1, 2026), and some hand out toys through schools, churches and agencies rather than to families directly.

Next step: On the website, find the campaign for your county and choose Request a Toy. If requests are closed, the page names the local groups that give out its toys.

Salvation Army Christmas help and Angel Tree

Many local Salvation Army offices give Christmas gifts to children in families going through a hard time.

Website

More about this: Salvation Army Christmas help and Angel Tree

The Salvation Army

Each office sets its own sign-up dates, and most close in the fall. Greater Cleveland's Christmas Assistance sign-up, for families with children 12 and younger, closed November 6, 2026.

Next step: Call your nearest Salvation Army office and ask whether Christmas sign-up is still open, or dial 211.

Checked against published sources November 18, 2026

Winter Crisis Program: shutoff or out of heating fuel

Help once each winter if your heat or electric is about to be shut off, is already off, or you are running out of fuel.

Call 800-282-0880Website

More about this: Winter Crisis Program: shutoff or out of heating fuel

Ohio Department of Job and Family Services

Open November 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027, for households at or below 175% of the federal poverty level. It can also help pay to start or transfer service.

Next step: Call to be connected to your local energy assistance provider, who takes the application.

Checked against published sources November 18, 2026

Keep the heat on for $175 (Special Reconnect Order)

Pay up to $175 to keep, restore or start gas or electric service this winter. There is no income limit.

Call 800-686-78261-800-686-PUCOWebsite

More about this: Keep the heat on for $175 (Special Reconnect Order)

Public Utilities Commission of Ohio

Runs October 12, 2026 to April 16, 2027, once per winter, for customers of electric and gas companies the PUCO regulates. A reconnection fee of up to $36 may be added. The utility puts the rest of what you owe on a payment plan. If your income qualifies, the Winter Crisis Program can pay the $175.

Next step: Call your gas or electric company and ask to use the Special Reconnect Order.
