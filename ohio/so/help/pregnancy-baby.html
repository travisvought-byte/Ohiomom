Booqasho kalkaaliso oo bilaash ah ka dib dhalashada ilmahaaga (Family Connects Ohio)

Kalkaaliso ayaa guriga kugu soo booqata, badanaa qiyaastii saddex toddobaad ka dib marka ilmuhu guriga yimaado.

Wac 800-755-47691-800-755-GROWBogga internetka

Wax badan oo ku saabsan: Booqasho kalkaaliso oo bilaash ah ka dib dhalashada ilmahaaga (Family Connects Ohio)

Family Connects Ohio, qayb ka mid ah Help Me Grow

Kalkaalisadu waxay hubisaa adiga iyo ilmaha, waxay ka jawaabtaa su'aalaha daryeelka ilmaha cusub, waxayna kugu xirtaa adeegyo kale. Waa bilaash, dakhligaagu wuxuu doono ha ahaadee, waxayna u furan tahay qoys kasta oo ku nool degmo ka qayb qaadata, oo ay ku jiraan qoysaska ilmo korsada iyo qoysaska ilmo ka dhintay.

Tallaabada xigta: Degmooyinka ka qayb qaadanayay Sebtembar 2025: Darke, Erie, Fayette, Greene, Guernsey, Hamilton, Huron, Mahoning, Noble, Shelby iyo Trumbull. Haddii aad ku nooshahay mid ka mid ah, ka codso booqasho Help Me Grow 12 toddobaad gudahood ka dib marka aad ilmaha guriga keento. Haddii degmadaadu aysan liiska ku jirin, wac oo weydii inay tan iyo markaas bilowday.

Doula uu Medicaid daboolo

Doula waa qof tababaran oo aan dhakhtar ahayn, kaas oo bixiya u-doodid iyo taageero shucuureed, jireed iyo waxbarasho inta lagu jiro uurka iyo dhalmada.

Wac 800-324-8680Khadka Medicaid ee dadweynahaBogga internetka

Wax badan oo ku saabsan: Doula uu Medicaid daboolo

Ohio Department of Medicaid

Laga soo bilaabo 3 Oktoobar 2024, Medicaid-ka Ohio wuxuu daboolaa adeegyada doula ee xubnaha uurka leh iyo kuwa dhawaan dhalay. Doula-du waa inay shahaado ka haysataa Ohio Board of Nursing.

Tallaabada xigta: Weydii qorshahaaga caafimaadka Medicaid liiska doula-yaasha shahaadada leh, ama wac khadka Medicaid. Doula-yaashu si isku mid ah ugama wada jiraan meel kasta, sidaa darteed bilow goor hore uurka.

Wada-shaqeeyayaasha deegaanka ee Cribs for Kids waxay qoysaska u qalma siiyaan sariir ilmo oo la qaadi karo oo bilaash ah, go' ku habboon iyo waxbarasho hurdo ammaan ah.

Wac 211Ohio 211

Wax badan oo ku saabsan: Meel bilaash ah oo ammaan ah oo ilmuhu seexdo (Cribs for Kids)

Ohio Cribs for Kids, iyadoo loo marayo waaxaha caafimaadka deegaanka iyo wada-shaqeeyayaasha

Ilmuhu wuxuu si ammaan ah u seexdaa isagoo keligiis ah, dhabarka u jiifa, sariirtiisa. Wada-shaqeeyayaashu waxay u adeegaan 71 ka mid ah 88-ka degmo ee Ohio, sidaa darteed waxay ku xiran tahay meesha aad ku nooshahay, agabkuna wuu dhammaan karaa.

Tallaabada xigta: Wac waaxda caafimaadka degmadaada oo weydii Cribs for Kids. Haddii aadan hubin cidda aad wacayso, wac 211 oo sheeg degmadaada.
