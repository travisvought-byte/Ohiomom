Protection orders: free form helper

A civil protection order is a court order telling an abuser to stay away.

Call 866-529-6446Legal aid: 1-866-LAW-OHIOWebsite

More about this: Protection orders: free form helper

Ohio Legal Help

This free tool asks plain questions and fills in the Ohio court forms for you. Filing has risks, so talk with an advocate about a safety plan first.

Next step: Use the form helper on a safe device. You do not need a lawyer to file, but legal aid may represent you for free.
