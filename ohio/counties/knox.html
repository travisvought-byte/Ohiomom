Knox County

Raising kids in Mount Vernon or anywhere in Knox County? Knox Public Health is the health department and a community health center, Kno-Ho-Co-Ashland Community Action is the community action agency, and the Winter Sanctuary is emergency shelter in the cold months. County seat: Mount Vernon.
