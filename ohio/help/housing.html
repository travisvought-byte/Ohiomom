More about this: Shelter phone lines in Ohio's biggest counties

Each county's homeless hotline

Columbus and Franklin County: the Homeless Hotline answers 24 hours a day. Cleveland and Cuyahoga County: Coordinated Intake answers weekdays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; at other times dial 211. Cincinnati and Hamilton County: the CAP line answers weekdays from 9 to 5, with shorter hours on weekends. Akron and Summit County, Canton and Stark County: each has one homeless hotline.

Next step: Call the line for the county you are in now. Anywhere else in Ohio, use COHHIO's county guide or dial 211.

More about this: Got an eviction notice? Start here

Ohio Legal Help and Ohio legal aid

A 3-day notice is the first step. If you have not moved out, your landlord can then file a case, and the court sets a hearing. Go to the hearing whether you plan to fight the eviction or to move.

Next step: Call, or use Find Your Legal Aid on the website, as soon as you get a notice or court papers.

COHHIO Housing Information Line

You can also email rentinfo@cohhio.org. Calls and emails are usually answered within two business days. The line explains the law. It does not give out rent money, find you housing or act as your lawyer.

Next step: Call or email with your question, and have your lease or notice in front of you.

Emergency help for families from your county (PRC)

Short-term help in a crisis for parents with low incomes and for pregnant women, from your county Job and Family Services office.

State fact sheet (PDF)

More about this: Emergency help for families from your county (PRC)

Ohio Department of Job and Family Services and county offices

The program is called Prevention, Retention and Contingency. Each county writes its own plan, so what is covered differs. The state names shelter, help moving away from domestic violence, disaster help and car repairs as examples, and some counties include back rent or a deposit.

Next step: Call your county Job and Family Services office and ask how to apply for PRC emergency help. Our county pages list the office where we have one.
