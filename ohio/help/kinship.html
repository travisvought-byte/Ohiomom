Raising a grandchild in Ohio

You took in the grandkids, maybe overnight. You are not alone, and you don't have to figure this out by yourself. Start with one free call to OhioKAN, then open what you need.

Start here

One free call to a navigator who knows every kinship program in Ohio, and a plan for your family.3 options

OhioKAN: a free navigator for kinship families

A navigator talks with you one on one, makes a plan for your family and helps you apply for what fits.

Call 844-644-65261-844-OHIOKANWebsite

More about this: OhioKAN: a free navigator for kinship families

Ohio Department of Children and Youth, managed by Kinnect

Free for Ohio's kinship and adoptive families. Navigators answer weekdays, 8:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., and can meet by phone, video or in person. They help with basic needs, benefits, child care, school, legal aid and family support, and they follow up to make sure you got what you need.

Sources: 1 2 3

Next step: Call and say you are raising a relative's child. You can also send a message through the website or ask for another way to be contacted, such as text.

Money for the children

Payments for kinship caregivers, and cash help in the child's own name.3 options

Kinship Support Program (KSP)

If children services placed the child with you, Ohio pays kinship caregivers a daily amount per child for a limited time.

How KSP works

More about this: Kinship Support Program (KSP)

Ohio Department of Children and Youth, through your county children services agency

It is for relatives and close family friends caring for a child in children services custody. Licensed foster caregivers, and caregivers who already have a custody order, cannot get KSP. You do not have to apply: the children services system finds the caregivers who qualify.

You cannot get KSP and Ohio Works First child-only cash for the same child at the same time.

Sources: 1

Next step: Ask the child's caseworker whether you are getting KSP and when it ends. When it ends, you may apply for child-only cash help.

Kinship Permanency Incentive (KPI): payments after you get custody

Once a court gives you legal custody or guardianship, Ohio can make a limited number of incentive payments for each child.

How KPI works

More about this: Kinship Permanency Incentive (KPI): payments after you get custody

Ohio Department of Children and Youth, through your county children services agency

Household income must be at or below 300% of the federal poverty level. A home assessment and background checks are part of it. Getting KSP first does not stop you from getting KPI later.

Sources: 1

Next step: Ask your county children services agency for the KPI application. Send it with your court order and proof of household income.

Medicaid and the doctor

Health coverage for the children, and the papers that let you consent to their care.1 option

Medicaid for the children in your care

Children in children services custody already have Medicaid. If you are raising them on your own, you can apply for Medicaid for the children.

Call 800-324-8680Medicaid Consumer HotlineApply

More about this: Medicaid for the children in your care

Ohio Department of Medicaid

Medicaid for the children does not cover you or other people in your home. A grandparent power of attorney or caretaker affidavit (below) lets a grandparent make health care decisions for the child.

Sources: 1 2

Next step: Apply through Ohio Benefits, the same application used for food and cash help, or call the hotline with questions.

Legal custody or a foster license? Free legal help to decide

Legal custody or guardianship comes from a court. A foster license comes through children services. Free legal aid can explain what fits your family.

Call 866-529-64461-866-LAW-OHIOWebsite

More about this: Legal custody or a foster license? Free legal help to decide

Ohio Legal Help and Ohio legal aid

You do not have to become a licensed foster parent to care for a relative's child. Licensing means a home study and training, and licensed relative homes get the same foster care payments as other licensed foster homes. With legal custody or guardianship you may qualify for KPI payments.

Sources: 1 2 3

Next step: Call legal aid, or make the kinship caregiver action plan above, before you sign anything in court.

If a parent is struggling with addiction

Many Ohio grandparents are raising grandchildren because of the opioid crisis. You can love your child and still keep the grandchildren safe. For yourself, see Taking care of you for people to talk to. If a child is in danger right now, call 911. Your county children services agency takes reports of abuse or neglect; your county page lists it where one is built.
