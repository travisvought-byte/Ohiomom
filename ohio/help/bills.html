Who to call

Ohio 211

One number for local help with shelter, rent, food and utility bills. Answered 24 hours a day.

Call 211Website

More about this: Ohio 211

Ohio 211 and local United Ways

In 2026 the state and Ohio 211 set out to bring the line to all 88 counties. In most counties you can also text your ZIP code to 898211.

Next step: Dial 211 and say what you need and what county you are in.

Help with heating and electric bills (HEAP)

HEAP helps households at or below 175% of the federal poverty level pay utility bills.

Call 800-282-0880Website

More about this: Help with heating and electric bills (HEAP)

Ohio Department of Job and Family Services

Crisis programs can help when you have a shutoff notice.

Next step: Call to be connected to your local energy assistance provider, who can take your application by phone or in person. The state's online application works on a computer only, not on a phone or tablet.

Ohio Benefits

WIC: food for pregnancy, babies and children under 5

WIC provides healthy food, formula help, breastfeeding support and health screenings for pregnant and postpartum women, infants and children up to age 5.
