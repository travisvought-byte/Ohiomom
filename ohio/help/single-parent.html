Parenting on your own in Ohio

Doing it on your own? Here is help paying for child care, opening or changing a child support case, free legal help with custody, and Head Start near you.

Who to call

Help paying for child care

Publicly Funded Child Care helps working or in-school parents pay for licensed child care.

Call 844-640-64461-844-640-OHIOWebsite

More about this: Help paying for child care

Ohio Department of Children and Youth

Families generally qualify at or below 145% of the federal poverty level.

Next step: Apply through the same Ohio Benefits application used for food and Medicaid, online or by phone.

Child support: open or change a case

Your county Child Support Enforcement Agency can establish paternity, set up a support order, collect payments and review an order when circumstances change.

Call 800-686-1556Website

More about this: Child support: open or change a case

Ohio Office of Child Support

Next step: Start on the state child support website or call the customer service line to reach your county agency.
