Help Me Grow Early Intervention

Ohio Department of Children and Youth

Ohio's free early intervention for children under 3 who are behind or have a disability. It is in all 88 counties, and visits usually happen at home. Parents can sign up their own child.

Next step: Call or fill out the online form, and a local coordinator will call you. If your child is almost 3, call now. This program ends at age 3, and the school district takes over.

Family and Children First Council

Pulls agencies together into one plan when a child needs help from more than one of them.

We have not confirmed a local listing for Franklin County yet. Start with the statewide contact below.

County Family and Children First Council

Ohio Family and Children First

When a child's needs involve several agencies at once, such as school, mental health, the county board of DD or children services, your county council can bring everyone to one table to make one plan. Some councils can also help pay for things like respite or mentoring.

Next step: Call your county's Family and Children First Council and ask for service coordination. Families can ask for it themselves.
