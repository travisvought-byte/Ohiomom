Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Thurston County, for children 3 and up8 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in Nebraska instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.

Head Start and Early Head Start

Free early learning in Thurston County2 places

Next step: Call and ask how to apply and what papers to bring. Programs can have waiting lists, so ask to be added even if there is no opening now.

Omaha Tribe Early Head Start

Free early learning in Macy, NE: Head Start for ages 3 to 5, and Early Head Start for babies, toddlers and pregnant moms. For families who qualify.

Call 402-837-4110

More about this: Omaha Tribe Early Head Start

Omaha Tribe of Nebraska

300 Chief St, Macy, NE 68039

This is a tribal program (American Indian and Alaska Native Head Start). Ask who can enroll. Free for families who qualify. Families with a low income qualify, and so do children in foster care and families who are homeless or who get TANF or SSI.
