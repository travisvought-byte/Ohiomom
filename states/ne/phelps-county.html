Phelps County, Nebraska

Raising kids in Holdrege or anywhere else in Phelps County? Here is who to call close to home. For public housing or a Section 8 voucher, the nearest agency we list is Kearney Housing Agency. Free legal help with custody, child support, protection from abuse or an eviction comes from Legal Aid of Nebraska. Expecting, or have a little one? You will find the health department near you. Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Phelps County, for children 3 and up8 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in Nebraska instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.
