Fremont County, Wyoming

Raising kids in Lander, Arapahoe or anywhere else in Fremont County? Here is who to call close to home. For public housing or a Section 8 voucher, the nearest agency we list is Rock Springs Housing Authority. Free legal help with custody, child support, protection from abuse or an eviction comes from Legal Aid of Wyoming, Inc. Expecting, or have a little one? You will find home visits for new parents near you. Low-cost clinics, free evaluations for your child and Head Start are below too. Pick what you need. Free, private, no sign-up.

Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Fremont County, for children 3 and up8 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in Wyoming instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.
