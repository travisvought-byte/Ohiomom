Capitol Planning Region, Connecticut

Raising kids in Hartford, East Hartford or anywhere else in the Capitol Planning Region? Here is who to call close to home. To apply for public housing or a Section 8 voucher, start with City of Hartford Housing Authority. Free legal help with custody, child support, protection from abuse or an eviction comes from Statewide Legal Services of Connecticut, Inc. Expecting, or have a little one? You will find home visits for new parents and free diapers near you. Low-cost clinics, free evaluations for your child and Head Start are below too. Pick what you need. Free, private, no sign-up.
