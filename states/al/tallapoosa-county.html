Tallapoosa County, Alabama

Raising kids in Alexander City, Camp Hill or anywhere else in Tallapoosa County? Here is who to call close to home. Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Tallapoosa County, for children 3 and up3 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in Alabama instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.
