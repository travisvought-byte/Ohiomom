🆘 Get help now

National Domestic Violence Hotline

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Free, private support any hour of the day. An advocate can help you think through safety, shelter and next steps, whether or not you are ready to leave.

Call, text or chat. You do not have to give your name.

Domestic violence help in Iowa

National Network to End Domestic Violence (NNEDV)

Iowa has a domestic violence coalition that knows the local shelters and programs. Shelter addresses are kept private for safety, so this site never lists them.

Call the National Domestic Violence Hotline any hour, or open the list and choose your state's coalition.

🧺 Food, bills and housing

SNAP in Iowa

Iowa SNAP agency, as listed by the U.S. Department of Agriculture

SNAP helps households with low income buy groceries. This page links to Iowa's SNAP website and phone number and tells you how to apply.

Open the Iowa entry, then apply online or call the number it lists.

Website

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: 1

WIC in Iowa

Iowa WIC agency, through the U.S. Department of Agriculture

Healthy foods, nutrition advice and breastfeeding support at no cost for women who are pregnant, recently pregnant or breastfeeding, and for babies and children under 5.

On the apply page, choose Iowa (or your Tribe) from the list. Then call or apply online with a WIC agency near you.

Website

Help with energy bills in Iowa

Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)

LIHEAP can help pay a share of heating and cooling bills. Each state runs its own program. Applying never costs a fee.

Call the National Energy Assistance Referral line, weekdays 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. Eastern, and give your city, county and Iowa.

NEAR hotline

Call 866-674-6327Website

Dial 211 in Iowa

211, United Way and partners

A free line that connects you to local help with housing, food, utilities, child care and more.

Dial 211, or search by ZIP code at 211.org. Say what you need and your county.

🍼 Pregnancy and new baby

Health coverage for your child in Iowa

Insure Kids Now, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

Medicaid and the Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP) cover many children in families with low or moderate income. Iowa sets its own rules.

Open the coverage page, choose Iowa, and follow the steps to apply.

1-877-KIDS-NOW

Call 877-543-7669Website

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: 1

Low-cost prenatal care near you in Iowa

HRSA health centers, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

Health centers charge on a sliding fee scale, so you can get care even if you are uninsured or cannot pay.

Search by ZIP code, call the center, and ask whether it provides prenatal care and what the sliding fee would be. Or call 1-800-311-BABY to find your area health department.

1-800-311-BABY

Call 800-311-2229Website

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: 1

National Diaper Bank Network member banks

🧩 My child learns or grows differently

Early ACCESS

Iowa early intervention program (birth to age 3)

Free early intervention for children under 3 who are behind or have a disability. You can call yourself. You do not need a doctor's note.

Call and ask for a free evaluation. If your child is 3 or older, call your public school district instead.

Call 888-425-4371Website

Checked November 8, 2026. Sources: 1 2 3 4

ASK Resource Center

Parent Training and Information Center

A free parent center for families of children with any disability, from birth to age 26. Staff help with school plans, meetings and your rights.

Call before your next school meeting.

Call 515-243-1713Website

Checked November 8, 2026. Sources: 1 2 3 4

Protection and Advocacy agency (disability rights)

The federally funded disability rights agency for your state. Call if your child with a disability is being denied services, education or fair treatment.

Head Start near you in Iowa

Office of Head Start, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

Free learning and development for children from birth to age 5 in eligible families. All programs welcome children with disabilities.

Enter your city or ZIP code in the locator, then call the program listed and ask how to enroll.

Free legal help in Iowa

LawHelp.org and the Legal Services Corporation

Legal aid offices help people with low income with custody, divorce, housing and benefits at no cost. Who qualifies depends on the office.

Use LawHelp.org to choose Iowa and find guides, forms and local contacts.

☕ Take care of me

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)

Free, private support for anyone who is pregnant or has had a baby and feels sad, anxious or overwhelmed. Counselors answer any hour, in English and Spanish.

Call or text. You do not have to be in a crisis to reach out.

1-833-TLC-MAMA

Call 833-852-6262Website

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: 1

Postpartum Support International

Postpartum Support International (PSI)

PSI has Support Coordinators in every US state who can talk with you about postpartum depression, anxiety and other struggles around pregnancy and birth, and point you to local help.

Call the HelpLine or open the PSI site and choose United States to find help in your area. This is not an emergency line. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

Call 800-944-4773Website

Parents Anonymous

Free, confidential emotional support for parents and caregivers when parenting feels like too much. Trained specialists answer by call, text or live chat any hour.

Call, text or chat. You do not have to be in a crisis.
