Cobb County, Georgia

Raising kids in Marietta, Smyrna or anywhere else in Cobb County? Here is who to call close to home. To apply for public housing or a Section 8 voucher, start with Housing Authority of the City of Marietta. Free legal help with custody, child support, protection from abuse or an eviction comes from Atlanta Legal Aid Society, Inc. Expecting, or have a little one? You will find the health department, home visits for new parents, breastfeeding support and free diapers near you. Low-cost clinics, free evaluations for your child and Head Start are below too. Pick what you need. Free, private, no sign-up.

Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Cobb County, for children 3 and up2 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in Georgia instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.
