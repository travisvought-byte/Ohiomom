Texas: help for moms

Raising kids in Texas and need a hand? Pick what you need below: Texas's own offices come first, then national lines that work anywhere. If your baby or toddler seems behind, Texas's early intervention program is Early Childhood Intervention (ECI), and you can call it yourself. For housing, legal aid and clinics close to home, open your county, such as Harris County or Dallas County, from the list below. Choose another state.
