Erie County, New York

Raising kids in Buffalo, Cheektowaga or anywhere else in Erie County? Here is who to call close to home. To apply for public housing or a Section 8 voucher, start with Buffalo Municipal Housing Authority. Free legal help with custody, child support, protection from abuse or an eviction comes from Neighborhood Legal Services, Inc. Expecting, or have a little one? You will find home visits for new parents and free diapers near you. Low-cost clinics, free evaluations for your child and Head Start are below too. Pick what you need. Free, private, no sign-up.

Free evaluation for your child

School districts in Erie County, for children 3 and up31 districts

From age 3, children can get special education free through the public schools, even before kindergarten. If you think your child has a delay or disability, call your school district and ask for an evaluation. If the district decides not to evaluate, it has to tell you why in writing. For a child under 3, ask early intervention in New York instead.

Next step: Call and ask for the special education office, or preschool special education for a child under 5. Say you would like your child evaluated, then make the same request in writing, with the date, and keep a copy.
